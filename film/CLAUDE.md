# film: the showcase film, *Many Hands* (moved out of the root CLAUDE.md by #755; from tools/showcase into this folder by Flint #1195)

This is the second half of the root file's "Offline capture" bullet; the first half (`--frame-step`, `--input-script`, `--pixels-per-point`, `--look`) is in `engine/crates/flint-player/CLAUDE.md`.

- **This folder is the whole film and only the film** (#722, Flint #1195): its set (`set.toml`, `beats/`, the generated `NN_<beat>.scene.toml`, `scripts/`), its shot list, storyboard, cut lists, score, synth and editor, side by side. Run the scripts from this repository's root (`python film/cut.py reel`); each finds this folder from its own path. The capture and verify steps are the engine's `flint capture` (`engine/crates/flint-cli/src/commands/capture.rs`, `engine/crates/flint-cli/CLAUDE.md`), and `film/film.toml` is a shot list in its format (`[capture]` + a `[[shot]]` per beat, scenes and input scripts named from this folder, `points_wide = 1920`, `out = "~/flint-film"`). `capture.py` and `compare.py` are gone. Anything a game could use belongs in the command, not here.
- The showcase film's rig: `flint capture film/film.toml [--shot <beat>] [--verify] [--scale 0.5]` captures each beat (scenes built from `film/set.toml` + `film/beats/*.toml` by `build_scenes.py`; run it after editing a fragment) to PNG sequences outside the repo; storyboard `film/storyboard.md`, book guides/filming-a-showcase.md. Its music (#438): `python film/music.py [cut] [--plot] [--verify]` arranges `score.py` to a cut list (`film/cuts/<cut>.toml`, shots in bars at 100 bpm, a bar = 2.4 s = 72 frames at 30 fps), synthesizes six stems (`synth.py`, no samples), renders the generated `music/<cut>.suite.toml` + `.events.toml` with `flint render-suite` and masters to -14 LUFS / -1.5 dBTP as `~/flint-film/music/many-hands-<cut>.wav`, exactly the cut's length. The same list drives the picture, one file and one editor per cut (#437, #593): `film/cuts/<name>.toml` (`master` 16:9 4K60, `reel` 9:16 30 fps; shots in bars, `tail_bars` where the last take runs on, `titles = "lower_third"|"caption"`, `points_wide`, `[encode]`, `[web]`): `python film/cut.py <name> --capture [--verify] [--dry-run] [--scale s]` writes `~/flint-film/<name>/shots.toml` (`cut_shot_list`: only the takes the cut uses, each as far as its last shot reaches, at the cut's size, rate and `points_wide`, absolute paths) and runs `flint capture` on it (`$FLINT`, else `target/release` or `target/debug`, else PATH), `python film/cut.py <name>` composes the titles in a worker pool and encodes with the WAV (master MP4, web MP4/WebM + poster; the reel and its story pieces), refusing a track whose `many-hands-<cut>.json` was scored to another list, and `check_cut.py <name>` checks every file's frames, its sound (length, -14 LUFS, true peak) and that every shot starts on the music's frame. The film's camera moves (`film::orbit`/`dolly`) reframe themselves in a portrait window (`film::portrait()`)
